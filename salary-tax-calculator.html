How is salary tax calculated?

Pakistan uses progressive slabs. Each rate applies only to the part of your income inside that slab. Take Rs 300,000 per month: annual salary is Rs 3,600,000, which falls in the 25% slab, so tax is Rs 316,000 + 25% of Rs 400,000 = Rs 416,000 per year, or about Rs 34,667 per month.

Think of tax like a staircase, not a cliff

Many people fear that crossing a slab means the whole salary gets taxed at the higher rate. That is not how it works. Imagine climbing a staircase: each step has its own price, and you only pay a step's price for the part of your income that stands on it. If you earn Rs 10,000 more and cross into the next slab, only that extra Rs 10,000 is taxed at the new rate. A raise always increases your take-home pay.

6 practical tips for salaried people

  1. Check your payslip every January. If payroll guessed your yearly income wrongly in July, you can still fix it across the remaining months instead of paying one big amount later.
  2. Changing jobs? Carry your old salary certificate. Each employer only sees its own payments, so tax is often under-deducted across a job change. Give the new employer your old certificate and the tax already deducted.
  3. Keep all deduction certificates. Bank, employer and utility withholding certificates make filing much faster and protect you if a notice arrives.
  4. Know the filing date. Returns are normally due on 30 September. Being late can cost far more than the tax itself.
  5. Keep one folder per tax year. Payslips, certificates, donation receipts and bank statements in one place saves hours later.
  6. Ask about pension fund and donation credits. Some contributions can reduce your tax. Rules change, so confirm with the FBR or a registered tax advisor.

Frequently asked questions

How much salary is tax free in Pakistan?

Annual taxable salary up to Rs 600,000 (Rs 50,000 per month) has no income tax under the FY 2026-27 slabs.

Does a raise reduce my take-home pay if I enter a higher slab?

No. Each rate applies only to the income inside its own slab, so extra salary always increases your take-home pay.

Do these slabs apply to freelancers?

No. Non-salaried individuals are taxed on a different, higher schedule. This calculator is for salaried individuals only.
