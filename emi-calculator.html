Loan EMI Calculator Pakistan

Plan your car, home or personal loan by checking the monthly instalment before you apply.

How the EMI formula works

EMI = P x r x (1+r)n / ((1+r)n - 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual rate / 12 / 100) and n is the number of months. This is the standard reducing-balance method used by most banks.

Banks may add processing fees, insurance and other charges, and Islamic banks use their own profit structures, so your bank's quote can differ slightly.

The 30% rule of thumb

A popular guideline is to keep all your monthly loan instalments below roughly 30-40% of your take-home income. Think of your income as a pizza: if loans eat more than a third of it, there is little left for food, bills and emergencies.

Tips before you take a loan

  • Compare at least three banks. A small difference in rate becomes a big number over several years.
  • Ask for the total cost. Processing fee, insurance, early settlement charges and late payment charges all matter.
  • Increase your down payment if you can. A smaller loan means less markup.
  • Pay extra when possible. Even one extra instalment a year can shorten the loan, but ask the bank about early repayment charges first.
  • Keep an emergency fund. Do not let the EMI be the reason you borrow again next month.

Frequently asked questions

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed amount you pay every month until the loan is fully repaid. It includes both principal and markup.

Does a longer tenure save money?

A longer tenure lowers the monthly EMI but increases the total markup you pay over the life of the loan.

Can I use this for Islamic financing?

It gives a good estimate, but Diminishing Musharaka and Ijarah are structured differently, so confirm exact figures with your bank.
