Electricity Units Calculator Pakistan

Add how many appliances you use and for how many hours to estimate your monthly units and cost.

How units are calculated

One unit is one kilowatt-hour (kWh). Units per month = watts x quantity x hours per day x 30 / 1000. Air conditioners, water heaters and irons use the most electricity, so reducing their hours has the biggest effect on your bill.

Watt values are typical averages. Check the label on your appliance for the exact figure. Your real bill also includes taxes and slab rates.

Where do your units actually go?

A quick example for one month using the default values above:

ApplianceUsageUnits / month
Inverter AC (900 W)8 hours a day216 units
Ceiling fan (75 W)10 hours a day22.5 units
LED bulb (10 W)6 hours a day1.8 units

One AC running 8 hours uses about as much electricity as 10 ceiling fans running 10 hours. That is why the AC is usually the first place to look when the bill feels too high.

10 ways to bring your bill down

  1. Set the AC to 26 degrees C or slightly higher; each degree lower makes it work harder.
  2. Clean AC filters every month so air flows easily.
  3. Switch old bulbs to LED.
  4. Use a fan together with the AC so the room cools faster.
  5. Keep the fridge away from walls and heat, and do not leave the door open.
  6. Iron clothes in one batch instead of a few pieces each day.
  7. Run the water pump only as long as needed.
  8. Unplug chargers and devices you are not using.
  9. Close doors and curtains in the afternoon to keep heat out.
  10. Read your meter on the same day each month to catch unusual jumps early.

Try this challenge

For one week, cut your AC time by one hour a day and note the difference in your meter reading. Small habits add up, and this calculator will show you the saving before you even try.

Frequently asked questions

What is one unit of electricity?

One unit equals one kilowatt-hour, which is a 1,000 watt appliance running for one hour.

Why is my real bill higher than the estimate?

Bills include taxes, fuel adjustments and slab-based rates, and some appliances use more power than the average values shown.

How can I reduce my electricity bill?

Use inverter appliances, switch to LED lighting, set the AC to a higher temperature and avoid running heavy appliances for long hours.
