Money tips that help every month

  • Pay yourself first. Move a small amount to savings the day your salary arrives, before spending.
  • Track one month of spending. Most people are surprised by where the money goes.
  • Know your numbers before big decisions. Check the EMI, the tax or the Zakat first, then decide.
  • Review your electricity use every season. Summer and winter habits are very different.
