Percentage formulas

X% of Y = X / 100 x Y. Percent share = X / Y x 100. Percent change = (New - Old) / Old x 100. These are useful for discounts, marks, salary increments, profit margins and more.

Cool trick: working backwards from a discount

A jacket costs Rs 800 after a 20% discount. What was the original price? The price you paid is 80% of the original, so original = 800 / 0.80 = Rs 1,000. Remember: divide by (1 - discount) to find the original.

Three mistakes to avoid

  • Increase then decrease is not equal. If a price rises 20% and then falls 20%, it does not return to the start. Rs 100 becomes Rs 120, then Rs 96.
  • Percent points are not percent. Going from 10% to 12% is 2 percentage points, but it is a 20% increase.
  • Always use the old value as the base. Percentage change is measured against where you started.

Mental math shortcut

To find 10% of any number, move the decimal one place left. For 5%, take half of that. For 15%, add them together. 15% of 2,500: 10% is 250, 5% is 125, total 375.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Multiply the price by the discount percentage and divide by 100. Subtract that amount from the original price.

How do I find percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100.

Can the percentage be more than 100?

Yes. If the new value is more than double the old value, the increase is above 100%.
