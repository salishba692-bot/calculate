About PakCalc

PakCalc is a free collection of calculators built for everyday financial questions in Pakistan. Our goal is to make salary tax, Zakat, loan and electricity calculations simple and accessible to everyone.

Calculators for everyday finances

Estimate deductions with our Pakistan salary tax calculator, check assets with the Zakat calculator, or compare borrowing costs with the loan EMI calculator.

Understand your results

Each tool includes formulas and examples. Calculations run in your browser, so your inputs stay on your device. Results are estimates for general guidance; confirm tax, financing and religious decisions with a qualified adviser. Report an error or suggest a calculator.
