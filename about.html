About PakCalc

PakCalc is a free collection of calculators built for everyday financial questions in Pakistan. Our goal is to make salary tax, Zakat, loan and electricity calculations simple and easy to understand.

We update tax slabs when new rates are announced, and each calculator explains its formula so you can check the result yourself.

Results are estimates for general guidance and are not a substitute for professional tax, financial or religious advice.
